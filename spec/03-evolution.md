# 03 — Biological Evolution and Spore-Inspired Stages (Phase 2)

## 1. Genetics

Every Kekborn has a unique permanent genetic identity. The procedural genetics system
supports: genome, inherited characteristics, mutations, physical and behavioural
attributes, intelligence, memory, perception, movement, strength, speed, environmental
adaptation, reproduction.

- A procedural creature-generation engine turns the genome into appearance and
  behaviour.
- Evolution happens through mutation and natural selection.
- Reproduction combines both parents with controlled random mutation.
- New species emerge over many generations (speciation by genetic distance).
- Ancestry and lineage are always preserved.
- All randomness comes from a seeded PRNG so runs are reproducible.

## 2. The five stages

Transitions depend on actual evolutionary and technological development (measured
thresholds in the simulation), never on timers.

**Stage 1 — Cell.** Explore a primordial environment, eat and collect energy, avoid
predators, develop basic physical traits, reproduce and mutate, compete for resources,
adapt to the environment. Players influence development.

**Stage 2 — Creature.** Larger world, hunting and gathering, limbs and physical
adaptations, interaction with other creatures, relationships, memory and behaviour
patterns, adaptation to different environments, visual creature editor.

**Stage 3 — Tribe.** Tribes, cooperation and competition, communication, leadership,
settlements, gathering and distributing resources, alliances and conflicts, traditions
and shared knowledge.

**Stage 4 — Civilization.** Agriculture, domestication, trade, economic systems,
specialized professions, cities and infrastructure, political institutions, laws and
governance, science and technology, diplomacy and warfare. Civilizations differ in
character and strategy. Leaders' traits shape government: a paranoid, greedy ruler
governs differently from a generous, diplomatic genius.

**Stage 5 — Space.** Advanced science and engineering, space exploration, planetary
colonization, resource extraction, interplanetary trade, contact with other
civilizations, competition and cooperation between advanced societies.
