# 09 — Security and Approval Requirements

These rules override every other part of the spec.

- No access to wallet private keys or seed phrases.
- No autonomous spending.
- No unapproved contract deployment.
- No unapproved token launch.
- No paid third-party APIs without approval.
- No external deployment without approval.
- No fabricated historical IMD data.
- No arbitrary AI-generated evolutionary outcomes.
- No NFT minting before project-owner artwork and metadata approval.

Every transaction needing funds, wallet authorization or deployment approval is
presented to the project owner, who is the final authority on Genesis artwork and the
collection. Recipients do not approve their artwork.

If a dependency blocks a task, report the exact blocker and continue with every
independent task.
