# 06 — Website and Player Experience (Phase 5)

A polished, playable browser game — not just a landing page or NFT gallery. Art
direction: painted medieval dynasty portraits of the Kekborn (see 01-genesis.md §5) +
Spore's sense of discovery + a detailed, dynastic character interface.

## Player dashboard
Connected wallet, Genesis Kekborn, rarity and contribution score, current stage, stats,
genetics, character traits, personality, family and dynasty, evolutionary history,
achievements.

## Character sheet
Portrait and animated creature; physical, mental and social stats; personality traits;
virtues and vices; congenital, learned and funny traits; relationships; family tree;
dynasty history; biography (generated from the event log, not invented); inheritance
info. Every trait has icon, name, description and gameplay effects; clicking a trait
explains how it affects the Kekborn.

## Creature interface
Visual creature editor, animations, genome and traits, ancestry and descendants,
reproduction history.

## World interface
Interactive map, ecosystems and environments, other creatures, tribes and settlements,
civilizations, resource distribution.

## Dynasty interface
Interactive family tree, ancestor and descendant profiles, inherited traits, famous
ancestors, dynasty reputation, rivalries, family achievements.

## Global statistics
Total Genesis Kekborn, living creatures, evolved species, extinct species, active tribes,
civilizations, discoveries, rarest genetic combinations, most common and rarest traits,
most successful dynasties, most ridiculous characters, most disastrous rulers.
