# 07 — Blockchain and Economy (Phase 6)

## 1. Blockchain architecture

Genesis collection as in 01-genesis.md. Future, designed but not built yet:

- Verifiable Genesis ownership.
- Permanent Genesis rarity and contribution metadata.
- Original Genesis traits and genetic identity.
- Optional breeding / offspring NFTs.
- Selected evolutionary achievements.
- Optional marketplace for creatures and items.

The simulation stays off-chain. The chain holds ownership, provenance and verifiable
events (e.g. periodic Merkle roots of world state). **No separate token without
explicit approval.**

## 2. Long-term economy (design only, do not implement)

Possible: creature marketplace, breeding rights, cosmetic traits, rare genetic
combinations, resources and crafting, inter-civilization trade, player-created items,
civilization economies.

Evolutionary success must not depend on spending. Value comes from gameplay, genetic
diversity, discoveries, personalities and unique civilizations. No speculative
financial mechanics without separate approval.
