# 08 — Technical Architecture, Execution Order, Deliverables

## 1. Modules (separate, documented APIs, versioned rules)

1. Simulation engine (deterministic tick loop, seeded PRNG).
2. Biological and genetic systems.
3. Character trait and personality engine.
4. Inheritance and dynasty system.
5. Creature behaviour and AI.
6. Evolutionary progression.
7. Social and civilization systems.
8. Persistent world and database.
9. Multiplayer infrastructure.
10. Player interface.
11. Blockchain integration.
12. NFT metadata and provenance.
13. Genesis artwork generation and approval dashboard.
14. Research and pattern-discovery agents.

Future agents must be able to add features without rewriting the project.

Character engine requirements: 300+ traits at launch; expandable trait database;
separate inherited / acquired / congenital / temporary categories; interaction and
compatibility rules; inheritance probabilities; dominant and recessive traits;
development through experience; relationship effects; dynasty tracking; reproducible
rules; balancing and versioning.

## 2. Execution order

Do not attempt the whole vision in the first cycle.

**Priority 1 — Genesis.** Investigate IMD data accessibility; take and preserve the
snapshot; contribution scores; rarity; artwork; initial traits and personalities;
metadata; approval dashboard with individual and collection-wide approval;
regeneration of rejected art; deployment authorization; mint and distribute.

**Priority 2 — Playable prototype.** Primordial world; Genesis Kekborn creation; basic
genome; ≥50 traits (full 300 designed); procedural creatures; character sheets;
personality and behaviour; feeding, movement, survival; reproduction and mutation;
natural selection; basic inheritance; persistent identity; multiplayer-ready
architecture; player dashboard.

**Priority 3 — Expansion foundation.** Modular architecture; persistent database;
evolutionary history; lineage; character and dynasty histories; inheritance engine;
relationships; research framework; specs for future agents.

**Priority 4 — Further development.** Creature, Tribe, Civilization and Space stages;
multiplayer interactions; advanced research agents; advanced dynasty mechanics;
optional economy.
