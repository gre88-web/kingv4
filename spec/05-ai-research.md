# 05 — AI and Autonomous Evolution Research (Phase 4)

## 1. AI analysis agents

AI agents may: analyse evolutionary behaviour, flag interesting genetic combinations,
detect unusual mutations, analyse social structures, identify emerging species,
investigate technological discoveries, compare civilizations, generate hypotheses and
propose new simulation experiments.

AI is an analytical and experimental layer on top of the simulation. **AI never decides
that a creature evolved or a civilization achieved something.** Every development comes
from the simulation mechanics.

## 2. Pattern discovery framework

- Run many independent worlds with different initial conditions.
- Preserve world seeds, rules versions and full histories.
- Compare evolutionary paths; find recurring genetic structures.
- Investigate why species survive; study cooperation vs competition.
- Analyse the emergence of intelligence and why civilizations rise or collapse.
- Measure how inherited character traits affect evolutionary success.
- A research agent continuously analyses results, proposes hypotheses and designs
  follow-up experiments.
- Every claimed discovery must be reproducible (seed + rules version + parameters)
  and independently testable.

Long-term goal: PEPEVERSE as an artificial laboratory for evolution, intelligence,
personality, inheritance, cooperation and civilization.
