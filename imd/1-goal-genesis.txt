PEPEVERSE Genesis snapshot and scoring. Research and data only: deploy, mint or spend nothing.

Source of requirements (read and implement against): https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gre88-web/kingv4/cbef28ee95096562e90693b0b9aeb169ed19d29e/spec/01-genesis.md (sha256 3b36c84e8185f5a068dcd5bbe710712ba24048ff3bfc4707cfad204b317472f6), sections 1-4 and 6.

Steps:
1. Fix a snapshot block on Ethereum and on Robinhood chain. Record block number and UTC timestamp for each.
2. Build the holder list for the IMD token (Ethereum 0xd34a99bc0f67ae1bbd63c660e6d0b0dd03e263b7, Robinhood 0x5f7bb59365ce557c26dbcaa4ee9d39a4b95b7127) with each wallet's balance at the snapshot block, from on-chain reads. Merge wallets across chains.
3. For every holder, collect IMD job history from api.imd.fun (requests/paid-by/:address, jobs/:id incl. paidBy, result, records, goals, seats, earnings). Only include orders created before the snapshot timestamp.
4. Classify each job: completed, abandoned, failed or ongoing; category (code/contracts, research, audit, website/product, oracle/indexer, multi-agent goal); scope; deliverables; quality signals. Count each job once; do not double count goal children.
5. Never guess. Where a job cannot be linked to a wallet, list it as unlinked and explain why.
6. Define a transparent Genesis Contribution Score from the factors in the spec. Balance alone must not be the score. Publish the formula and a script that recomputes it from the raw data.
7. Assign five rarity tiers (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary) from the score distribution itself (e.g. natural breaks), not fixed percentages. Explain the cut points.
8. Derive genetic tendencies per wallet from job categories per the mapping table in the spec.
9. Output: raw source responses, holders.csv, jobs.csv, scores.csv (wallet, balance, jobs by status and category, IMD spent, score, tier, genetics), methodology.md, a data-accessibility report listing what is available, what is missing and alternative verification, and a manifest with sha256 of every file.

The snapshot is final once written. Treat it as immutable input for all later work.
